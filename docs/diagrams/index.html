Each figure carries its authored content beside it — .source.js for the three engine-rendered patterns and the flow, .figure.js for the two bespoke figures, .data.js for the state spine. Source advances when the authored content changes; the renderer advances only when its realization does.

Every figure exports on demand from its own HUD, in the resolved theme — PNG page for the chromed render and PNG diagram for the canvas alone. Two figures additionally keep one committed raster, because their governing prose embeds it directly; the PNG action on those two cards opens that committed file. The HTML and its authored content remain canonical, and the rasters are supplemental. See README.md for the full contents list and the refresh cadence each class follows.
