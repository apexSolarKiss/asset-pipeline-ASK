## What Milestone 12 Marks

The plateau identity is a single load-bearing claim:

**Across three structurally-distinct architectural seams — Zone 6 intake-time judgment trace, intake-time reference rationale, Zone 4 reuse-time governed-output governance — the same architectural-pressure pattern has now landed independently: design surface → operational pressure → high reconstruction success → carrier-shape sharpened-not-closed. The recurrence is itself the architectural finding. Subsequent pressure should be a fresh strategic decision against the multi-axis state, not auto-chaining from the most recent loop.**

This is more than aggregation. The architecture's stance has shifted from "Zone 6 / Option F has saturated as a single arc" (milestone 11) to "the design-surface + operational-pressure + sharpened-not-closed pattern has now recurred at three independent seams." The recurrence is no longer a sequence of in-flight pressure tests on one carrier family; it is a portfolio-level observation that subsequent pressure can build on.

The plateau is bounded honestly:

- No carrier-shape choice across the three axes is closed. R-C vs R-D (intake-reference-rationale), Z4-C vs Z4-D (reuse-time governance), and PR #256's three contradiction-resolution alternatives (Option F) all remain held.
- Operational support across all three axes is at one-reader / one-substrate / one-mocked-shape depth. The recurrence pattern itself has not been pressured by multi-reader replication, cross-substrate generalization, growth-pressure substrates, or symmetric-alternative-mocked tests on any axis.
- The reader-judgment signals (Q-9 for Z4; Q-11 for intake-reference-rationale; the cross-test contradiction-resolution signal for Option F) are diagnostic, not implementation proof.
- All Apex Named Limitations carried forward. All held schema candidates carried forward. No held-candidate adjudication.

## The Three-Axis Recurrence as a Single Pattern

The plateau's defining structural observation.

Pre-PR #263, the architecture's stance was: milestone 11 absorbed the Zone 6 / Option F arc as a coherent foundation; the carrier-shape choice within Zone 6 was sharpened, not closed; subsequent pressure was a fresh strategic decision.

Across PR #263 through PR #271, two further architectural seams (intake-reference-rationale and Zone 4 reuse-time governance) were independently pressured using the same surface-then-test pattern that produced milestone 11's Zone 6 plateau. Both axes converged on the same outcome:

1. **A design surface** naming carrier-shape alternatives with one rejected as prose-only-insufficient and several structurally-suggested in distinct roles.
2. **An operational pressure test** mocking one of the structurally-suggested alternatives (R-D for intake-reference-rationale; Z4-C for Zone 4) within a hybrid posture.
3. **High reconstruction success** at one-reader / one-substrate / one-mocked-shape depth (11/11 for intake-reference-rationale; 9/9 for Zone 4).
4. **A reader-judgment diagnostic signal** characterizing the mocked carrier as sufficient at this depth, with the unmocked alternative remaining materially plausible.
5. **A carrier-shape choice sharpened, not closed** with explicit framing that the test cannot support "X beat Y" or "Y is unnecessary" or "Y is required" — direct comparison was not run.

This recurrence — across architecturally-distinct objects (intake-time judgment trace vs intake-time reference rationale vs reuse-time governed-output governance) — is the milestone 12 finding. The pattern is consistent, the outcome is consistent, and the held state is consistent. No single test closes its respective carrier-shape choice; the recurrence does not authorize closure across axes either.

## What Is Earned

Carried forward through milestone 12:

- **Three-axis recurrence as a pattern.** Design surface → operational pressure → high reconstruction success → sharpened-not-closed carrier choice now appears across three architecturally-distinct seams.
- **Carrier-attached rationale / governance beats prose-only across multiple seams.** Z4-A and R-A and Option-A-equivalent all rejected on the same architectural grounds — three readers independently flagged the failure mode across PR #254 / #258 / #260, and the architectural extension to both intake-reference-rationale (PR #263) and Zone 4 (PR #268) recovered the same rejection.
- **Design-surface-first pattern validated.** Naming alternatives across required / structurally-suggested / held / rejected before testing carries through cleanly into the operational pressure step; the labeling vocabulary is reusable across distinct architectural objects.
- **Operational pressure confirms reconstructability before schema.** All three axes earned operational support without authoring entity placement, field types, cross-entity relationships, or rollup patterns.
- **High reconstruction success does not equal carrier-shape closure.** 11/11 and 9/9 reconstruction success did not close R-C vs R-D or Z4-C vs Z4-D respectively. Reconstruction success measures whether the mocked shape is sufficient; it does not adjudicate between the mocked shape and structurally-suggested alternatives.
- **Sharpened-not-closed is now an architectural state, not indecision.** The state has explicit framing rules established across PR #266 and propagated forward: only the mocked carrier shape is tested; alternatives are reader extrapolation; the test cannot support "X beat Y" or "Y is unnecessary" or "Y is required."
- **Reader diagnostic signals are useful but not implementation proof.** Q-9 (Z4) and Q-11 (intake-reference-rationale) and the cross-test signal for contradiction-resolution all contributed pressure-shape information; none authorized a specific implementation choice.
- **Direct comparison remains separate from reconstruction success.** No symmetric test (R-C-mocked or Z4-D-mocked) has been run. The sharpened-not-closed state can only be resolved by direct comparison, not by additional asymmetric reconstruction tests.

## Why This Is Not Implementation Readiness

The recurrence is an architectural finding, not an implementation signal. Specifically:

- **No carrier-shape choices closed.** R-C vs R-D held; Z4-C vs Z4-D held; Option F contradiction-resolution among three alternatives held. Without closure on at least one of these, downstream entity placement cannot be authored.
- **No fields authored.** Each axis's mocked shape used illustrative sub-fields, list values, and cross-references; none has been promoted to schema, normalized for entity placement, or pressured for field-type discipline.
- **No entity placement earned.** Whether each carrier shape sits on an existing entity, a new entity, an annotation, or a lookup chain is a held question across all three axes.
- **No validator consumer defined.** No downstream code path or workflow consumes the YAML appendix or any of the three axes' constraint vocabularies as orchestration input.
- **No orchestration interface defined.** The "machine-readable vs machine-usable" gap named at milestone 8 / 9 remains open across all three axes' carrier vocabularies.

Milestone 12 makes the multi-axis state legible. It does not change the implementation-readiness state.
