This artifact attempts a model: apply Phase 1's normative-force criterion set to brand-system content from the four landed Phase 2 deepenings plus the sparse-articulation fallback pressure conditions, and surface what each of milestone 9's three live shapes for the brand-system layer carries, distorts, and fails to carry. It does not commit the architecture to a shape, propose a schema, or authorize any mutation. The brand-system layer structural decision remains held per milestone 9; this artifact lays the surface against which the decision will eventually be earned or further refined.

## What This Artifact Attempts

Milestone 9 named the brand-system layer's status as a "named unresolved structural decision" with three live shapes:

1. **First-class structural carriers throughout** — the IA gains structural homes for brand platform, brand-wide constants, photography conventions, and applied guidelines.
2. **Terminal operator-mediated translation** — the IA explicitly accepts prose + operator judgment as the resolution path for upstream normative content; downstream packet / slot carriers absorb what intake produces.
3. **Hybrid** — structural carriers for stable brand-wide constants; operator-mediated judgment for abstraction, conflict, sparsity, override, and application.

Milestone 9's posture: "hybrid most likely on current evidence." That posture has been functioning as a placeholder. The Phase 1 normative-force pass v1 ([`docs/normative-force-and-durable-layer-pressure-v1.md`](normative-force-and-durable-layer-pressure-v1.md)) produced a criterion set the architecture didn't have at milestone 9: six candidate kinds of normative force (descriptive / directive / constraint / reference / judgment / governance) with carrier-status observations grounded in three operational pressure points. With that criterion set, the three live shapes become testable against concrete brand-system content rather than compared abstractly.

The exit criterion: the architecture knows what each of the three live shapes carries, distorts, and fails to carry against current evidence. The decision itself remains held.

## Why This Surface Now

Three convergent pressures named the moment:

- The four landed Phase 2 deepenings (photography style guide; asset library; brand platform; visual identity system) consistently surfaced the brand-system layer's unmodeled status from multiple angles (articulated visual conventions; demonstrated visual evidence; non-visual articulation-only; concrete brand-wide constants). Two-angle pressure became three-angle and then four-angle pressure.
- Phase 1's force-kind criterion set named non-adjudicatively in §Implications for Held Items / Brand-system layer that "the force model suggests why a hybrid answer is likely" without specifying which carriers should mutate.
- Continuing input-category deepenings 5 and 6 (application guidelines; past campaigns) would likely surface the same gap from more angles without resolving the carrier-shape question.

Further deepening before surfacing the carrier decision is more pressure-on-the-same-gap. The decision-surface attempt is the next move that can either earn or deflate the milestone-9 hybrid placeholder.

## The Three Live Shapes (Tested)

Per milestone 9, only the three named shapes are in scope. The artifact does not introduce a fourth formal shape. If criterion application surfaces additional shape candidates, they are treated as either a hybrid variant or as evidence that the three-shape framing is insufficient — not as new options.

### Shape A — First-class structural carriers throughout

The IA gains structural homes for brand platform, brand-wide constants, photography conventions, and applied guidelines. Every input category lands in dedicated structural carriers; operator judgment is bounded to filling structural fields per intake protocol.

### Shape B — Terminal operator-mediated translation

The IA explicitly accepts prose + operator judgment as the resolution path for all upstream normative content. Brand-system intake produces prose absorbed into downstream packet / slot carriers; structural carriers in the IA serve packet-and-below content, not brand-system-layer content.

### Shape C — Hybrid

Structural carriers for stable brand-wide constants (the VIS-deepening territory); operator-mediated judgment for abstraction, conflict, sparsity, override, and application (the brand-platform-deepening territory + the sparse-articulation fallback pressure conditions).

## Category Examples

The examples are illustrative, not exhaustive. They walk one or two input categories per shape to show what the table's claims mean against concrete content. Cross-mode behavior remains observation, not commitment.

### Shape A against VIS-deepening content

The VIS deepening surfaced logo references, typography assignments, color codes, and grid systems as articulated brand-wide constants. Under Shape A, these would land in dedicated structural carriers (a `brand_constants` entity with fields for logo URLs, typeface assignments by usage, hex codes by role, grid system parameters). The content's natural shape is already structured (URLs, names, hex codes, parametric math); Shape A captures this cleanly.

Now extend Shape A to brand-platform content from the brand platform deepening. Positioning, voice, values, audience are prose-rich abstractions. Shape A would commit the architecture to fields like `value_propositions: array<string>`, `voice_attributes: enum`, `audience_targets: array<segment_ref>`. Two concrete distortions: (1) the enum vocabulary differs per brand (one brand's "warm" voice attribute does not map cleanly to another's "approachable" — the structural commitment forces cross-brand vocabulary unification that hasn't been earned); (2) sparse articulation collapses to empty fields without graceful fallback — when a brand has logo + color articulated but no voice articulation, the structural fields make upstream silence look like missing data rather than upstream silence.

### Shape B against brand-platform-deepening content

The brand platform deepening surfaced abstraction-heavy content (positioning, voice, values, audience) with structural void at brand-system layer. Under Shape B, this content lands as prose translated by operator judgment into packet-level prose carriers (`business_intent`, `creative_intent`, `bounded_creative_discretion`). The translation preserves the prose-rich nature of the content; operator does the abstraction-to-application work. This is the strongest case for Shape B.

Now extend Shape B to VIS-deepening content. Logo references, typography assignments, color codes — Shape B would translate these into packet-level prose ("use the brand wordmark in the upper-left at 16% canvas width"; "use the brand display typeface for primary headings"; "color palette restricted to the brand's warm-neutral set"). Two concrete distortions: (1) cross-packet consistency relies on operator discipline rather than structure — if 50 packets need the brand wordmark URL, the URL is prose-absorbed 50 times with no audit trail; (2) the natural shape is thrown away — a hex code carried as prose is harder to consume programmatically than a hex code carried as a structured field.

### Shape C against asset-library + brand-platform content

Asset library content (approved photography; prior campaigns; master files) already lives in `reference_assets` + lookup chain via existing structural carriers. Brand-platform content (positioning, voice, values, audience) is operator-translated into packet-level prose. Under Shape C, both patterns continue; the architecture commits to a boundary that respects each category's content shape.

The boundary itself is the open question. The asset-library deepening showed that visual reference content is "the input category most directly served by existing structural carriers." The brand-platform deepening showed the opposite at the brand-system layer. The VIS deepening showed an intermediate case: articulated visual constants that don't fit either side cleanly. Shape C's commitment is "different content gets different carriers based on shape" — but the line-drawing criterion has to be specified, and at this evidence depth the criterion is "what carries cleanly elsewhere in the architecture under the Phase 1 force-kind decomposition." That criterion is implicit, not first-class.

### Stress-condition behavior under sparse / contradictory / divergent articulation

The sparse-articulation fallback pressure note surfaced that operator judgment becomes load-bearing under stress conditions, and that inferred-not-stated remains a prose discipline rather than a structural carrier. Shape A's failure mode under stress is documented above (sparse structural fields; resolution-before-fill). Shape B handles stress gracefully because the fallback chain is its native resolution mechanism. Shape C handles stress as gracefully as Shape B in the operator-mediated zone, but inherits the same intake-time judgment-trace gap — when an operator resolves contradictory brand-platform content under Shape C, the resolution leaves prose in packet carriers but no structural trace of which fallback chain position produced the resolution.

## Cross-Shape Synthesis

Reading across the three shapes against the criterion set:

- **No single shape carries the criterion set cleanly.** Shape A breaks under sparse / contradictory / divergent articulation — the stress conditions the architecture has named as load-bearing for real brand intake. Shape B under-uses naturally structural content (logo references; color codes; typography; approved reference assets) and loses cross-brand consistency at the constants level. Shape C carries articulated content cleanly *and* handles stress conditions gracefully — but inherits Shape B's intake-time judgment-trace gap that has no current carrier.
- **The intake-time judgment-trace gap is the third zone.** Shape A obscures it; Shape B accepts it as operator-mediated without trace; Shape C surfaces it as a zone that neither structural carriers nor pure operator-mediated prose handles cleanly. The milestone-9 held candidates (`derivation_basis`, `articulation_state`, `fallback_chain_position`, `contradiction_log`) are paper-pressed structural-carrier candidates for exactly this zone. Their earn-or-deflate status sits in the third row of the normative-force pass v1 conversion criteria ("require operational pressure before promotion").
- **The criterion for line-drawing under Shape C is implicit at this evidence depth.** The Phase 1 force-kind decomposition supplies it: content whose force kind currently carries cleanly elsewhere in the architecture (descriptive force at SKU scope; reference force; governance force; seam-level judgment trace) earns structural carriers at the brand-system layer; content whose force kind is prose-carried elsewhere (packet-level directive; packet-level constraint; reasoning content) stays prose-carried at the brand-system layer; content whose force kind has no current carrier (intake-time judgment) is the third zone.

## What the Surface Implies if Hybrid Remains the Surviving Shape

This section reads only as implications, not as recommendations or schema. The structural commitment remains held; the artifact does not move it.

If hybrid is the surviving shape — and the comparison surface above suggests it is the only shape that doesn't fail cleanly somewhere — the evidence implies the hybrid would likely separate three zones of content:

1. **Stable brand-wide constants + approved references → structural carriers.** Logo references, typography assignments, color codes, grid systems, approved photography, prior campaigns. The VIS-deepening + asset-library evidence shows existing structural-carrier patterns already work here.
2. **Prose-rich directive content → operator-mediated translation.** Positioning, voice, values, audience, brand evolution, exception handling. The brand-platform-deepening evidence shows operator translation preserves the prose-rich nature of the content; existing pattern at packet level extends naturally upstream.
3. **Intake-time judgment trace → currently uncovered.** Operator resolution of sparse / contradictory / divergent articulation produces downstream prose with no structural trace of which fallback chain position produced the resolution. The milestone-9 held candidates are paper-pressed for this zone; their structural form remains unearned.

The implications about earning the third zone:

- Earning intake-time judgment carriers requires operational pressure — either operational brand intake (Phase 5 of the originating plan, held) producing concrete cases where the absence of trace becomes load-bearing, or a separate operational test designed to pressure intake-time judgment.
- Without earning the third zone, hybrid is a two-zone commitment with operator discipline carrying the third zone in prose — usable, but with the accountability gap named.
- The criterion for line-drawing between zones 1 and 2 (which content earns structural carriers vs which gets operator-mediated translation) is implicit at this evidence depth via the Phase 1 force-kind decomposition. Making the criterion first-class would be a separate move.

The surface tells the architecture: hybrid is plausibly the surviving shape; its commitment shape would have three zones; the third zone has no current carrier and would either need to be earned operationally or accepted as operator-mediated without structural trace. The decision itself — whether to commit to hybrid, whether to earn the third zone first, whether to accept the two-zone version — remains held.
