## Curation Semantic Split

The architecture had been collapsing two senses of "curation" into one concept:

- **Authorship-bearing discretion** — where creative judgment actually happens; site varies per mode and per packet. In SKU-mode evidence (PKT-SKU-007 / 009 / 010), it appears concentrated at the curation seam. Collection-mode evidence (PKT-COLL-001) does not contradict that placement at the depth tested, but did not pressure the semantic split as directly as PKT-CAMP-004. In campaign-mode evidence at PKT-CAMP-004, authorship surfaced upstream at prompt-authoring and prompt-remediation.
- **Governance-bearing curation** — where output becomes governed asset; located at the curation seam in every observed mode; carries five-axis provenance + curator + `curation_pattern`.

These may co-locate (SKU evidence appears to) or separate (campaign at PKT-CAMP-004 did). The split is now first-class in `docs/architecture.md`.

The split does not resolve the curation-premise fork. It clarifies the vocabulary the fork is being judged against.

## Brand-System Layer Status

The brand-system layer's unmodeled status is reframed from "limitation" to "named unresolved structural decision."

Two-angle pressure from Phase 2 deepenings makes the gap harder to defer:

- The brand platform deepening (Phase 2 third) pressured from the abstraction angle: positioning, voice, values, audience — almost entirely prose-carried with structural void.
- The VIS deepening (Phase 2 fourth) pressured from the concrete-constants angle: logo references, typography assignments, color codes, grid systems — brand-wide articulated constants with no first-class IA home.

Three shapes remain live:

- **First-class structural carriers throughout** — the IA gains structural homes for brand platform, brand-wide constants, photography conventions, and applied guidelines
- **Terminal operator-mediated translation** — the IA explicitly accepts prose + operator judgment as the resolution path for upstream normative content
- **Hybrid (most likely on current evidence)** — structural carriers for stable brand-wide constants; operator-mediated judgment for abstraction, conflict, sparsity, override, and application

The decision is not pre-resolved. It is named.

## Bounded `refines` Reading

Phase D §3.1 falsified `holds` at PKT-CAMP-004 scope: all three curation events were `confirmation-bearing`, and authorship-bearing work surfaced upstream. This falsifies the *unqualified* architecture-wide claim that curation always carries runtime authorship.

Phase D §4 + §7 recommended `refines` as the current best bounded read at PKT-CAMP-004 scope: upstream authorship-bearing moments shape the candidate set; curation ratifies or rejects.

Phase D §9 preserved `fragments` as a named falsification path: a packet where upstream authored moments occur but do not flow into the slot's curation event would weaken `refines` in favor of `fragments`. Not doctrine.

SKU evidence (PKT-SKU-007 / 009 / 010 full-flow) continues to support curation as a strong authorship-bearing governance seam. Collection-mode evidence (PKT-COLL-001) does not contradict that pattern at the depth tested, but did not pressure the semantic split as directly as PKT-CAMP-004. The singular-curation framing remains useful in SKU mode; provisional architecture-wide.
