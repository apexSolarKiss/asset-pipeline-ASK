# Milestone 5 // SKU-Driven Furniture v1 Prototype Plateau

This note records the plateau where SKU-driven Furniture v1 reached a layered prototype state across output slots, visual-reference binding, and thin capture / governance identity.

## What This Milestone Established

- `output_slots` are first-class expected output obligations
- packet-level generation exposed mapping pressure
- slot-level generation proved cleaner for role-specific output production
- text-only invariants improved coherence but were structurally insufficient for set-level visual binding
- URL-reference paths surfaced through lookups did not act as visual conditioning in this Airtable surface
- image-bearing attachment reference, the Gate 2 path, is the visual binding surface for SKU-driven Furniture v1
- the earned three-input v1 pattern:
  - `slot_prompt`
  - `packet_reference_image_lookup`
  - `product_image_lookup_lookup`
- thin governed-asset identity bridge from raw slot output to `generated_assets` record works through the existing schema
- B-evidence promotion boundary and flat `slot_status` lifecycle remain in force
- capture / governance mechanics is paused at the thin-bridge plateau pending a concrete reopen trigger

## What It Did Not Establish

- production-readiness for SKU-driven Furniture v1
- cross-mode generalization to collection / marketing / campaign workflows
- final schema doctrine
- `asset_family`, `asset_applications`, or `generation_attempts` schema
- durable `asset_uri` capture, because raw Airtable attachment URL writes remain tool-layer-limited
- fuller capture provenance, such as `capture_reason`, capture timestamp, source attachment ID as a first-class field, or capture notes
- product-truth drift resolution
- D-probe execution

## What Remains Out Of Scope

- reopening any closed chain artifact
- retroactive mutation of historical evidence, including `PKT-SKU-007`, `PKT-SKU-008`, or `PKT-SKU-009`
- cross-mode application without separate evidence / authorization
- production-readiness claims
- architecture rewrite

## Next Pressure Point

Whether a concrete governance use case emerges that earns deferred provenance work, such as downstream consumer needs, audit trail, multi-author review, repeat capture event, or delivery / export, or whether the plateau can hold while attention pivots to other thesis-relevant work such as product-truth D-probe, cross-mode investigation, or external positioning.
