# Resolution Calculus — Evidence Boundary v1

## Posture

```text
evidence-boundary artifact, not architecture expansion
records what a failable test proved and what it explicitly did NOT prove
not a schema, not a validator, not a new primitive
not method-doctrine expansion
not canonical adoption of the tested vocabulary
not a second test, not a generalization of the proof
self-superseding if a later test revises the boundary
```

This artifact absorbs one accepted evidence result at boundary depth. It does not re-derive or canonize the model under test; the full model is not reproduced here. Only the **evidence boundary** is absorbed: what held, and what remains unproven.

## What was tested

A failable resolved-specification test (A1) pressured a **slot-resolution calculus** — how the inputs that converge at slot resolution (per [`docs/visual-payload-architecture-v2.md`](visual-payload-architecture-v2.md)) resolve when they conflict. The tested model, in brief: conflicts resolve by **statement-level binding posture, authority, and override policy** (not by source category and not by order) into five terminal states — *resolved · judgment-required · authorization-required · input-required · unsatisfiable* — with references kept distinct from their interpretations and unsatisfiable results emitting a core. Thirteen deliberately-conflicting closed-world cases, blind, two independent runners.

## What held (the earned boundary)

- **12 of 13 cases converged** to the pre-registered partition across both runners, with **no forbidden moves**: no silent invention, no interpretation inheriting a reference's authority, no profile-order precedence, and a correct unsat core.
- The 13th case was a **fixture under-specification** (the exception authority's presence was unstated), not a calculus failure — both runners reasoned identically and surfaced the gap. The fixture is corrected (state the authority present → authorization-required).
- **Earned, bounded:** the calculus is *determinate and usable at the tested depth.*

## What it did NOT prove

```text
not universal across substrates, brands, or conflict shapes (one synthetic substrate, two runners)
not a schema or a runtime
not adopted as canonical architecture vocabulary
the calculus held — no revision earned
```
