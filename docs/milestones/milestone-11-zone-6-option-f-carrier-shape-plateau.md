Across PR #250 through PR #260, the arc has produced:

1. **Empirical evidence of the failure mode** under continuity break against no carriers (PR #250).
2. **A surviving conceptual posture** (Option F: hybrid inspection log + intake decision record) capable of addressing all six trace dimensions PR #250 surfaced (PR #252).

The architecture's stance on Zone 6 has shifted from "held conditional pressure path" to "conceptual posture operationally supported across three substrates with structured carrier-shape constraints and a sharpened-but-not-closed implementation choice." This is a coherent architectural foundation. Subsequent pressure on Zone 6 can begin from this foundation rather than re-establishing the conceptual posture.

## Carrier-Shape Choice: Sharpened, Not Closed

Per PR #256, the contradiction-resolution carrier shape was held among three structurally-suggested alternatives:

1. **Sub-field on intake decision record** — operationally supported across PR #258 + PR #260; PR #260 surfaced the duplication-under-1:many structural pattern (Event 2 duplicated across D1's and D2's contradiction_resolution sub-fields)
2. **Separate `contradiction_log` entity** — untested; PR #260's surfaced duplication strengthens the case for this alternative as natural-fit-for-1:many (one event record with foreign-key references to multiple affected decisions, rather than duplication of event content)
3. **Annotation on inspection log entry** — untested; PR #260's source-internal-inconsistency case (Event 2's brand_platform.audience-carried + brand_platform.positioning-rejected) suggests this alternative as natural-fit-for-cross-layer relationships at the inspection layer

The choice is **sharpened** by PR #260's structural signal but **not closed** because all three alternatives remain materially plausible. PR #260's test does not differentiate them on reconstructability-of-trace; the discriminating factors are structural (duplication overhead vs entity proliferation vs cross-layer annotation complexity), and the test substrate's complexity did not force a structural collapse on any of the three.

Plateau-marker stance: the choice is held with sharper structure than before, not deferred or pending resolution. The architecture explicitly does not promote any carrier shape to schema; the held state with three operationally-defensible alternatives is the doctrine, not a placeholder.
