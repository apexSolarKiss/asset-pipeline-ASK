# Brand-Ingestion Authority: Propagation and Binding (v1)

## Posture

```text
bounded architecture pressure / authority note
not schema · not carrier · not structured IA v3 · not validator / runtime
not a re-enumeration of the six brand-system input categories
does not revise any existing intake artifact in place
does not authorize Airtable, client, or prototype work
self-superseding when a first external brand engagement pressures it
```

## What this is

A bounded note on one seam: **when brand evidence becomes a statement downstream packets and
slots may treat as operative.**

The intake architecture models how brand-system content is extracted, ranked, and distributed
into carriers. It does not model where the authority to *bind* that content comes from. The
question is not whether a statement is accurate. It is whether anyone with standing has adopted
it, at what scope, and for how long.

This note names the authority structure and marks what is earned. It authors no carrier.

## Two paths, not one

The architecture's non-identities prevent object collapse — an extracted claim is not the source
statement it came from. They do **not** imply that every transition breaks authority. Transmission
of an already-authorized rule is different in kind from newly composed content, and collapsing the
two either imposes redundant re-approval on literal brand rules or hides the materially larger
burden a synthesis carries.

```text
A // AUTHORITY-PRESERVING TRANSMISSION

source statement already operative under a pre-existing, locatable
authority relation at the required scope
+ faithful transmission
+ provenance and limiting context preserved
>>
continues to operate under that existing authority, within the preserved scope
```

```text
B // AUTHORITY-CONFERRING ADOPTION

any proposition or operational instruction not already authorized at the
required scope — synthesis · inference · contradiction resolution · override ·
operator-authored content · use beyond a source statement's existing authority
→ evidentiary validation
→ semantic confirmation or correction
→ adoption and binding by an authorized actor, at a declared scope and effective period
>>
operative under that authorization relation
```

Judgment does not propagate through synthesis; a synthesis inherits authority only through
validation, correction, and binding. The paths are distinguished by what the statement is and
what the act did — not by who handled it. An AI-performed faithful transmission of a client's
stated palette rule is path A. An operator's careful inference from an asset library is path B.

### Verification does not create authority

The discriminator is not whether a client conversation occurred. It is what the act did:

```text
confirm a pre-existing authority relation, without changing proposition, scope,
priority, effective interval, or permitted downstream use
>>  verification evidence — path A remains

create · renew · revive · extend · narrow · override ·
select between competing authorities · authorize a new use
>>  authorization-bearing act — path B
```

"The 2025 visual-identity guide remains our current global digital standard through December
2026" evidences a relation that already existed. "Use the older guide for this campaign," or
"treat the palette you inferred as our rule," creates or changes authority.

**One utterance can contain both limbs. Classify each limb, not the meeting.**

Where the distinction cannot be established, the statement is unresolved —
`authorization-required` — and does not travel under path A by default.

## Two independent axes

Nonjudgmental is not the same as mechanical. Model-generated interpretation is not deterministic
and not exact; it simply carries no normative authority on its own. Keeping the axes separate
lets the architecture say so without either overstating machine reliability or pretending
interpretation did not occur.

```text
operation character   transmission · interpretation · synthesis · decision
authority effect      preserves · proposes · validates · binds · fails
```

The boundary inside operation character is **not textual exactness**. It is whether
decision-relevant meaning changed:

```text
TRANSMISSION — may preserve authority

transcription · translation · normalization · unit conversion · field mapping,
where modality, scope, exceptions, priority, effective interval, and
decision-relevant meaning remain unchanged and independently checkable

INTERPRETATION / SYNTHESIS — proposes only

any operation that selects among plausible meanings, resolves ambiguity, drops a
qualifier, composes a new operational rule, resolves contradiction, or adds
content not entailed by the authorized source statement
```

This matters for machine-usable form specifically. Rendering an authorized rule into a structured
representation is not automatically a new decision; a verifiably lossless normalization can carry
the source's authority intact. What cannot is a normalization that quietly resolves an ambiguity
or drops a qualifier the source depended on.

Only a decision by an authorized actor binds. The cells are not free: a synthesis cannot preserve
an authority it did not carry in.

## Validation and authorization are events, not properties

Authority is not an attribute a statement possesses. It is a relation established by an act, and
the act is what carries the scope and the expiry. A statement is operative *under* a given
authorization, never operative full stop.

Each such act is locatable along these dimensions:

```text
actor
authority or delegation basis
proposition
evidence basis
decision
scope
effective interval or review trigger
supersession or withdrawal
downstream use
```

Conceptual dimensions, not fields. Whether they warrant a carrier is a separate question this
note does not answer.

**One authorized decision may confirm meaning, adopt the proposition, and bind its scope in a
single act.** Semantic confirmation, adoption, and binding are distinguishable functions; they
are not mandatory sequential gates. Requiring serial events would import production-side
topology as ceremony and would reduce no decision-relevant uncertainty.

## Role defaults

Roles are functional and may coincide. What must be locatable is the delegation, not the job
title.

- **An AP operator has no client normative or binding authority merely by being the operator.**
  A client may explicitly delegate a bounded normative-validation or decision-making role; that
  delegation, its scope, and its recourse must be locatable.
- **Subject-matter expertise is not authorization.** A person may be an expert, an authorized
  decision-maker, and the decision owner at once, or hold only one of those.
- A computational agent may occupy an authorized-decision-maker role only by the functional test
  in the doctrine; current model sampling does not meet it.

## Fallback precedence is not authority

`asset library carries`, `newer carries`, and `scope-specific carries` are evidence-ranking rules.
They order candidate propositions for validation. They do not confer authority — unless an
authorized client has adopted them as standing delegated policy, which is itself an authorization
act and is then locatable as one.

The operator-judgment protocol's Case 2, in which the operator marks a client's articulated brand
value as performative-or-aspirational and lets asset-library evidence carry, crosses this seam
without naming which condition applies. Under ranking it is a proposal; under delegated policy it
is an authorized substitution. The architecture does not currently say which, and the difference
is what a client would want to know.

## Authority trace is not derivation trace

The intake dry run's five trace dimensions — presence-of-inspection, articulation-state,
chain-position, derivation-basis, contradiction-resolution — record how content was derived.
They remain scale-conditioned: operator continuity bridges them at one-operator scale, which is
the architecture's standing and correct read.

Authority evidence is different in kind. Once an external party owns the decision, operator
continuity cannot substitute for evidence of what that party adopted, because the operator is not
the party. The obligation begins with the first external authority holder, not with the second
operator.

Adjacent authority vocabulary already exists downstream. The governance record carries authority,
provenance, permissions, acceptance, and authorized exception for a governed asset; the held
rights-and-permitted-use family carries territory, duration, and licensing. Its applicability —
and whether it is sufficient — for brand-system statements remains unmodeled.

## Authority is scoped, dimensional, and orthogonal to certainty

- Visual-direction authority does not establish release, licensing, synthetic-alteration, legal,
  or publication authority. These are separate apertures with separate owners.
- A statement may be operative for one campaign, category, territory, or period and unauthorized
  outside it. Carried outside its scope it is not a weaker authority; it is unauthorized there.
- A client may knowingly authorize a provisional reading. Uncertainty must stay visible, but it
  does not negate an otherwise clear authorization act.

## What the live prototype shows

The Integrated Four-Workflow Home-Goods base supports the seam at carrier-shape depth only.

`constraint_rules` already carries brand-level rules structurally: four active records at
`scope_type = brand_context`, `scope_value = Warm Restraint`, each with `active_status` and
`priority`. So rule scope and prototype activation state have carriers. Source currency — whether
the originating authority remains current, who declared it so, and for what effective interval —
and the authority relation itself do not.

**The live records show that a rule can be active downstream while the system cannot establish
whether it was faithfully transmitted from a still-current authority-bearing source or newly
inferred and adopted.** `BC-WR-PALETTE` reads identically either way. Path A and path B are
indistinguishable in live state, which is the seam stated concretely rather than abstractly.

`workflow_packets.creative_intent` and `bounded_creative_discretion`, and
`output_slots.slot_prompt`, are multiline text. One packet carries `Decision owner: ASK as
marketing curator-of-record` inside `bounded_creative_discretion` prose, which shows prose can
hold decision ownership for a local synthetic case. `review_gates` holds no records and is
package-facing.

This earns conceptual articulation. It does not earn Airtable or schema work.

## Non-identities this note preserves

```text
validation ≠ authorization
verification of an existing relation ≠ creation of one
fallback precedence ≠ delegated authority
authority trace ≠ derivation trace
visual authority ≠ legal / release / publication authority
authorization relation ≠ proposition
statement ≠ downstream inheritance outside authorized scope
nonjudgmental ≠ mechanical
lossless transmission ≠ textual exactness
```

## Adjacent: authorization does not repair coverage

An authorization gate cannot repair omitted evidence or an artificially narrowed set of candidate
propositions. Where an AI pass shapes which sources are read and which propositions are formed,
the resulting statement may be duly authorized and still rest on an unrepresentative field.

This belongs first to source coverage and corpus adequacy, which
[`docs/brand-intake-acquisition-and-blind-safety-v1.md`](brand-intake-acquisition-and-blind-safety-v1.md)
already governs: state what a corpus exercises and what it omits before interpreting results.
Recorded here as an evidence-boundary warning that meets this seam, not as a claim about
bounded generativity.

## Earned and held

**Earned.** The authority structure above: two propagation paths, bounded by whether an authority
relation pre-existed; the two axes, bounded by decision-relevant meaning rather than textual
exactness; validation, adoption, and binding as locatable acts that may co-locate in one decision;
role defaults stated as delegation rather than occupation; and the authority-trace obligation
beginning at the first external authority holder.

**Held.** Whether any of it warrants a first-class carrier. An approved brief, a decision record,
or other locatable evidence may carry the authority adequately until operational pressure proves
otherwise. No held trace candidate is adjudicated, and the intake artifacts stand unchanged.

**Not claimed.** Operational proof. The central claim — that validation, adoption, and binding
are acts an external authority holder performs distinguishably — has not been pressured, and cannot be by a
synthetic fixture, because a fixture has no independent authority to withhold.

- Revises no existing intake artifact; the layered intake architecture, the cross-category

- Re-enumerates no input category and creates no workflow mode or architectural layer.
- Does not claim brand intake is an instance of the bounded-generativity topology.
- Does not require selection and binding to be separate gates.
- Authorizes no Airtable mutation, prototype run, package build, or client engagement.

## Self-superseding clause

Superseded by: a first external brand engagement that pressures whether validation, adoption, and
binding are separable in practice; a decision record or brief that carries the authority relation
adequately without a carrier; an operational test that earns or deflates an authorization carrier;
or a later architecture pass that absorbs authority propagation into the definition layer.
