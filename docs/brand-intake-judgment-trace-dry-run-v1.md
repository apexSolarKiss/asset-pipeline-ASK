This artifact runs one bounded operational pressure test: walk a fresh synthetic brand-system stack through the [sparse-articulation fallback pressure note's](brand-discovery-sparse-articulation-fallback-pressure-v1.md) six-layer fallback chain under a sparse-articulation condition, and surface what operator judgment is required at each moment, what trace that judgment leaves with current carriers, and whether the trace is sufficient for downstream architectural legibility. The exit posture is observation, not specification: the walk earns / deflates / readouts the milestone-9 intake-trace held candidates against fresh operational pressure.

## What This Artifact Attempts

Phase 2's brand-system hybrid carrier decision surface ([`docs/brand-system-hybrid-carrier-decision-surface-v1.md`](brand-system-hybrid-carrier-decision-surface-v1.md)) named the third zone of the hybrid implication: intake-time judgment trace, currently uncovered. Phase 1's normative-force pass v1 ([`docs/normative-force-and-durable-layer-pressure-v1.md`](normative-force-and-durable-layer-pressure-v1.md)) listed the milestone-9 intake-trace candidates (`derivation_basis`, `articulation_state`, `fallback_chain_position`, `contradiction_log`) in the third conversion-criteria row — "require operational pressure before promotion."

This artifact applies that pressure. The single question:

> When brand-system input is sparse, what operator judgment is required, what trace does that judgment leave, and is that trace sufficient for downstream architectural legibility?

The artifact's expected outcomes are bounded to three classes, named in the originating plan:

1. **Earn operationally**: one or more intake-time judgment-trace candidates surfaces as load-bearing in the walk
2. **Prose discipline sufficient at current evidence depth**: existing carriers (prose absorption into packet / slot fields; `slot_evaluation_notes`) carry the trace sufficiently for the architecture to remain legible
3. **Unmodeled pattern**: the walk surfaces a judgment-trace dimension not cleanly covered by the named held candidates

All three are architecturally productive outcomes. The walk does not pre-commit to any.

The intake task to anchor the walk: produce a one-packet workflow for a hero product image of a single SKU (the synthetic stack's existing product category — kitchen textiles). The packet needs `business_intent`, `creative_intent`, `bounded_creative_discretion`, packet-level `approved_reference_keys`, and one or more `output_slots`. The operator must resolve these from the synthetic stack via the six-layer fallback chain.

## The Walk: Six-Layer Fallback Chain Under Sparse Articulation

The six-layer fallback chain (from the sparse-articulation pressure note): asset library → photography style guide → brand platform → intra-category recency / specificity → Phase 1 worked-example defaults → operator-marked inferred-not-stated.

The walk records, at each layer, what operator judgment is required and what trace is left in the architecture's current carriers.

### Layer 1 — Asset library

The four reference images are inspected. Operator judgment required: do they converge on a coherent aesthetic? On what axes (composition; lighting; surface treatment; cropping)? Which images, if any, are usable as packet-level references?

In this walk: the four images converge on warm-tone product-isolation studio shots; diverge on background treatment (two have neutral seamless; two have soft mineral surface). Operator decides to use all four as packet-level references with a prose note about background variation.

Trace currently available:
- `reference_assets` structurally carries each image
- `approved_reference_keys` on the packet links them
- The operator's *inspection* (that the four were examined and read as convergent on tone, divergent on background) is not structurally traced — it could be captured in prose in `slot_evaluation_notes` if the operator commits to writing it, but is not load-bearing in any current field

Trace-gap observation: the operator's *inspection act* and the *axes of convergence/divergence* leave no first-class structural trace. The downstream packet inherits the references without a record of how they were judged.

### Layer 2 — Photography style guide

Not articulated in the synthetic stack. Operator confirms absence.

Trace currently available: none. There is no structural mark of "this layer was checked and was empty" vs "this layer was not checked." The packet's downstream prose carriers absorb whatever the operator pulls from later fallback positions, with no upstream indication that Layer 2 contributed nothing because it was absent.

Trace-gap observation: presence-of-inspection trace is absent. Held candidate `articulation_state` flag (sparse / contradictory / generic / divergent / absent) would cover this dimension at the *layer* level if earned.

### Layer 3 — Brand platform

Audience field articulated ("everyday households"). Voice, values, positioning absent.

Operator judgment required: extract the articulated audience cue. For the absent fields (voice, values, positioning), decide whether to infer from asset library (Layer 1 evidence) or to leave as inferred-not-stated and fall through to a later layer.

In this walk: operator lifts the audience cue verbatim into the packet's `creative_intent` as one prose sentence. For voice/values/positioning, operator infers a "calm, warm-tone, accessible" qualifier from the asset library's convergent tone and writes it into `creative_intent` as inferred.

Trace currently available:
- The packet's `creative_intent` carries both the lifted-verbatim audience cue and the inferred voice qualifier as combined prose
- Distinguishing "lifted from brand platform" from "inferred from asset library convergence" is not structurally captured; the operator could write "(inferred from references)" inline as prose discipline, but is not load-bearing
- The packet's `bounded_creative_discretion` could carry the inferred dimension as a separate bounded clause, but the architecture has no convention here

Trace-gap observation: derivation-basis trace is absent. Held candidate `derivation_basis` field on prose carriers would cover this dimension if earned.

### Layer 4 — Intra-category recency / specificity

No prior packets in the kitchen-textiles category exist in this synthetic instance (the packet under construction is the first). Operator confirms absence and falls through.

Trace currently available: same as Layer 2 — no structural mark that Layer 4 was checked-and-empty. The packet has no "this is the first in its category" flag; downstream readers cannot distinguish "operator skipped Layer 4" from "Layer 4 was empty."

Trace-gap observation: chain-position trace is absent. Held candidate `fallback_chain_position` annotation on resolved content would cover this if earned. Held candidate `worked_example_defaults` first-class carrier would partially overlap.

### Layer 5 — Phase 1 worked-example defaults

The architecture's Phase 1 worked-example sketches carry default aesthetic content per mode. For SKU-driven mode, the SKU-driven sketch carries packet-level patterns. Operator decides whether to import these defaults.

In this walk: operator applies the SKU-driven sketch's hero-slot pattern (clean studio; product isolation; mild context). The pattern is absorbed into the packet's `creative_intent` and slot `slot_prompt` as prose.

Trace currently available:
- Same as Layer 3 — prose absorption into packet / slot carriers, without structural trace of "this content derived from Phase 1 worked-example defaults at fallback chain position 5"
- The packet has no link to the Phase 1 sketch that informed it; downstream readers can read the resulting prose but cannot reconstruct which fallback layer produced which clause

Trace-gap observation: same as Layer 4. The chain-position trace plus derivation-basis trace combine here — the operator made two distinct judgment moves (chose Layer 5 over Layer 6; applied SKU-driven sketch defaults), neither of which leaves a load-bearing structural trace.

### Layer 6 — Operator-marked inferred-not-stated

For content still unresolved after Layers 1–5, operator marks as inferred-not-stated. In this walk, no content reached Layer 6 — Layers 1, 3, and 5 produced enough content (combined with operator inference) to fill the packet. Layer 6 would have applied if, say, no asset library content existed.

Trace currently available: prose discipline (operator writes "inferred:" or "derived:" in slot_evaluation_notes or in creative_intent prose). No first-class structural carrier.

Trace-gap observation: the walk did not pressure Layer 6 directly because Layers 1–5 absorbed the sparsity. But the cumulative effect of Layers 1–5 was that the packet's prose carriers contained a mix of lifted-verbatim, inferred-from-evidence, and worked-example-default content — all of which is currently indistinguishable in `creative_intent` prose without operator discipline.

## Trace-Gap Synthesis

Across the six-layer walk, the operator made at least six distinct judgment moments. Five trace dimensions surfaced:

1. **Presence-of-inspection trace** — at each layer, did the operator inspect the layer? Currently absent. Not covered by any named held candidate. (May compose into `articulation_state` if that candidate is interpreted broadly.)
2. **Articulation-state trace** — at each layer, was the layer articulated, partially articulated, generic, or absent? Currently absent. Covered by milestone-9 held candidate `articulation_state` flag.
3. **Chain-position trace** — for each piece of resolved content in the packet, which fallback chain position produced it? Currently absent. Covered by milestone-9 held candidate `fallback_chain_position` annotation.
4. **Derivation-basis trace** — for each piece of resolved content, was it lifted verbatim, inferred from evidence (which evidence?), or applied as a worked-example default? Currently absent. Covered by milestone-9 held candidate `derivation_basis` field.
5. **Contradiction-resolution trace** — when sources conflict, how was conflict resolved? Not pressured by this sparse-only walk. Covered by milestone-9 held candidate `contradiction_log` entity.

What current carriers provide: the packet's prose carriers (`business_intent`, `creative_intent`, `bounded_creative_discretion`, `slot_prompt`, `slot_evaluation_notes`) absorb whatever the operator pulls. The trace is per-packet, in prose, without dimensional decomposition. Reading a packet downstream cannot distinguish lifted content from inferred content, cannot identify which fallback layer produced which clause, and cannot reconstruct the operator's inspection pattern.

Where downstream legibility holds: at one-operator scale, with the operator carrying memory of the intake decisions, the prose carriers are sufficient because the operator can re-narrate the trace verbally when asked. The architecture's current carriers do not pretend to capture the trace; the trace lives in operator continuity.

Where downstream legibility breaks: at any scale beyond one operator — multi-session intake without operator continuity, team intake, agentic intake, future cross-brand reuse — the trace is structurally absent. A reader returning to the packet without intake-session context cannot reconstruct what was lifted vs inferred vs defaulted vs absent.

## Outcome Read

Against the three pre-named outcome classes:

### Outcome class 1 — Intake-time judgment-trace candidates surfaced as conditionally load-bearing

The walk surfaces three milestone-9 held candidates as conditionally load-bearing if the architecture exceeds one-operator continuous context:

- `articulation_state` flag at the layer level (covers dimension 2)
- `fallback_chain_position` annotation on resolved content (covers dimension 3)
- `derivation_basis` field on prose carriers (covers dimension 4)

Each maps to a judgment moment that recurs at every fallback chain position. The walk does not operationally earn these candidates, because multi-operator / continuity-breaking scale was not tested directly. It surfaces the scale condition under which they would likely become load-bearing and names the operational pressure needed to earn or deflate them.

### Outcome class 2 — Prose discipline sufficient at current evidence depth

At the architecture's current evidence depth (one-operator project; ASK as single curator across the project's lifetime), prose discipline carries the trace sufficiently because operator continuity provides the bridge that structural carriers would otherwise have to provide. The walk does not deflate the held candidates — it leaves them as a conditional pressure path bound to a scale condition the walk did not test directly.

The honest read: at current evidence depth, Outcome class 2 carries. Prose discipline is sufficient under one-operator continuous context. Outcome class 1 remains a conditional pressure path: if the architecture needs to operate beyond operator continuity, the same walk suggests these candidates would become load-bearing, but that scale condition has not yet been operationally tested.

### Outcome class 3 — Unmodeled judgment pattern

The walk surfaces one trace dimension not cleanly covered by the named held candidates: presence-of-inspection trace (dimension 1 above) — whether and how the operator inspected each layer regardless of what the layer produced. Currently uncovered; could compose into a broadened reading of `articulation_state` (if interpreted to include "layer not inspected" as a distinct value) or could surface as a separate candidate.

The walk also surfaces, more weakly, that derivation-basis trace (dimension 4) wants sub-classification: lifted-verbatim vs inferred-from-evidence vs worked-example-default vs unresolved-marked. `derivation_basis` as a single field would need an internal vocabulary to carry this; currently held as an undifferentiated field. This is sub-classification pressure on a held candidate, not a separate unmodeled pattern.

Contradiction-resolution trace (dimension 5) was not pressured by this sparse-only walk and remains held without earn or deflate.

## What the Walk Suggests (Implication Voice)

The walk suggests that minimum trace would need to answer:

- whether each fallback chain layer was inspected
- the articulation state of each layer at inspection time
- which fallback chain position produced each piece of resolved content
- whether each piece was lifted, inferred (and from what), or defaulted

Whether this minimum trace should be structurally represented or carried in disciplined prose is an architectural decision the walk does not make. The decision depends on the scale assumption the architecture commits to (single-operator continuous-context vs multi-operator or scale-breaking).

What the walk does *not* suggest:

- any specific field shape for the held candidates
- any specific schema for intake-time trace
- any specific UI or intake-form design
- any commitment to whether the structural form is earned or deflated

The structural decision remains held per milestone 9.

## What This Artifact Does Not Do

- Resolve the brand-system structural decision
- Commit the architecture to any carrier shape for intake-time judgment trace
- Authorize any held candidate (`derivation_basis`, `articulation_state`, `fallback_chain_position`, `contradiction_log`, or others)
- Propose schema mutation
- Authorize Airtable mutation
- Specify minimum-trace fields, schema, intake form, or UI design
- Walk contradictory or divergent stress conditions (sparse-only at v1; contradictory / divergent remain unpressured)
- Test multiple synthetic stacks (one stack at v1)
- Test scaled operator conditions directly (the multi-operator scale appears only as a conditional in the outcome read; not operationally pressured by the walk itself)
- Pressure marketing-mode or campaign-mode-specific intake conditions
- Extend the structured IA model v1 appendix
- Authorize subsequent phases of the originating plan (held-candidate adjudication map; architecture front-door absorption; reader / navigation condensation; method.md bridge update; external article audit)

The walk's value is providing fresh operational pressure on intake-time judgment trace that the architecture previously had only paper-pressed. Its limits are bounded by current evidence at one walk on one synthetic stack under one stress condition.

## Self-Superseding Clause

This artifact is a v1 single-walk dry run. It should be superseded by:

- A contradictory-articulation or divergent-articulation walk that pressures the `contradiction_log` candidate
- A multi-operator or scale-breaking operational test that operationally earns or deflates the conditional pressure path Outcome class 1 surfaced
- A subsequent walk on a different synthetic stack or against a different mode (marketing / campaign / cross-base) that surfaces patterns the home-goods sparse case does not
- A subsequent artifact that resolves the architecture's scale assumption — whether single-operator continuous-context is the architecture's commitment or whether scale beyond that is in-scope
- A subsequent milestone marker that absorbs the walk's findings into the architecture front door

It does not supersede the structured IA model v1, the apex definition-layer artifact, the Phase 1 mode sketches, the cross-mode synthesis, the four Phase 2 deepenings, the sparse-articulation fallback pressure note, milestone 8, milestone 9, the campaign-mode fork-resolver arc, the non-Airtable extraction attempt v1, the normative-force pass v1, or the brand-system hybrid carrier decision surface v1. Those remain authoritative for their subject matter; this artifact reads them at operational-pressure depth and walks one bounded test.
